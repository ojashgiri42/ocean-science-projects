This script shows the topography and seafloor elevation of Western Pacific (Japan and the Mariana Trench) where the data was extracted from a NOAA server. ETOPO 2022 was used as it combines ocean soundings, satellite altrimetry, and land topograpphy into a unified resolution grid.
Firstly we get the data from the server, then we isolate the the elevation data and slice the lat and lon of our proposed region. 
We apply all negative depth (bathy <0) condition and turn the positive land values into blank(NaN). 
We use (pcolormesh) to get high resolution grid of ocean depths and use ('Blue_r') to make contour blue shades where shallow water are light blue and deep water trenches dark blue. 
Finally we lock the color scale between 8000m to 0m with ( vmin=8000 and vmax=0) to be able to interpret the map.
