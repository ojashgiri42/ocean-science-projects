This script detects and categorizes Marine Heatwaves (MHWs) using a synthetic 30-year daily Sea Surface Temperature (SST) time series, following the framework of Hobday et al. (2016, 2018). Marine heatwaves warm ocean events causing mass coral bleaching, kelp forest collapse, and marine die-offs. MHWs helps to establish by baseline and identify periods where temperatures exceed the local 90thpercentile threshold for at least 5 consecutive days.
Firstly we generate 30 years of daily timestamp from 1993 to 2022. We add seasonal_cycle, warming_trend, random noise and an injected_anomaly (June-July 2021) to build the synthetic SST.
Secondly, we compute climatological mean and 90th perentile baseline by year with the function groupby("doy") for every day of the year. 11-day centered window filter was used to smooth out daily random noise. (is_above_p90) flags days where daily SST exceeds the local 90th percentile threshold. (cumsum()) assigns a unique numerical ID to every consecutive stretch of exceedance days. we then add Hobday et al. duration criterion of 5 days which filters out transient 1-4days warm spikes.
Finally, we compute hobday intensity bands and plot hydrograph to visualize and evaluate the duration and severity of extreme marine heatwave events.
