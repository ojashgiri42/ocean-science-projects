This script uses synthetic (analytic) vertical profiles ($0\text{--}1000\text{ m}$) of the kind measured by Biogeochemical Argo (BGC-Argo) floats to illustrate the Biological Carbon Pump. No float data is used. Photosynthetic phytoplankton in the sunlit surface ocean fix carbon and release oxygen. As organic matter sinks into the dark twilight zone ($200\text{--}1000\text{ m}$), bacteria respire it, consuming dissolved oxygen and releasing nutrients like nitrate. Oceanographers measure Apparent Oxygen Utilization (AOU)—the difference between theoretical oxygen saturation and actual dissolved oxygen—to quantify this subsurface organic matter remineralization.
Firstly we simulate physical and biogeochemical vertical profiles of temp, salinity, chlorophylla, nitrate and O2. Then we calculate oxygen saturation and apparent oxygen utilization (AOU). The oxygen saturation formula in the code is a simple linear placeholder, not the Garcia & Gordon (1992) solubility equation. 
Secondly, as we are trying to visualize 4 parameters we design a multi-panel figure i.e Hydrography(Temp & Salinity), Chlorophyll-a, Carbon Export & AOU shading. (sharey=True) helps to synchronize the vertical depth axes for all four panels. (.twiny()) overlays salinity on a second top x-axis while sharing the primary temperature y-axis. (fill_between) fills the area along the vertical depth profile for chlorophyll-a and AOU.
Lastly we invert all the fig which helps to visualize it better , matching standard ocenographic profile conventions.
